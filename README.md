# Nassau Candy Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Nassau Candy dataset to understand customer behavior, profitability, product performance, regional performance, shipping patterns, and business opportunities.

The analysis was performed using Python and Pandas in Google Colab.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance
- Identify profitable customer segments
- Analyze customer retention and order frequency
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products
- Analyze regional profitability
- Evaluate shipping performance
- Analyze division-level profitability
- Identify opportunities to increase sales and profit
- Provide data-driven business recommendations

## Dataset

- Transaction Records: 10,194
- Unique Customers: 5,044
- Columns: 21
- Missing Values: 0
- Duplicate Rows: 0

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Jupyter Notebook

## Customer Insights

- 55.73% of customers are One-time customers.
- 43.14% are Repeat customers.
- Only 1.13% are Loyal customers.
- Repeat customers contribute 64.70% of total gross profit.
- Loyal customers generate the highest average profit per customer.
- The 1→2 order retention rate is 48.06%.

## Product Insights

- The top 5 products generate approximately 95% of total gross profit.
- Wonka Bar - Scrumdiddlyumptious is the largest individual profit contributor.
- Nutty Crunch Surprise has a strong 71.35% gross margin.
- Kazookles has a very low 7.69% gross margin and requires investigation.

## Regional Insights

- Pacific is the strongest region by sales and total profit.
- Interior has the highest regional gross margin at 66.43%.
- Gulf has the lowest absolute sales and profit and represents a potential volume-growth opportunity.

## Division Insights

Chocolate is the dominant division and contributes approximately 95.06% of total gross profit.

The Other division has a significantly lower gross margin of approximately 44.84% and requires further investigation.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase One-time customer conversion into Repeat customers.
2. Target high-value Repeat customers for Loyal conversion.
3. Protect and promote the top five profit-driving products.
4. Investigate low-margin products such as Kazookles.
5. Investigate the low-margin Other division.
6. Protect Pacific's strong performance and focus on increasing sales volume in Gulf.

## Limitations

The conversion and profit opportunity calculations are scenario estimates rather than guaranteed future results.

The analysis identifies associations between customer behavior and profitability but does not prove causation.

The dataset represents a specific period and may not reflect future customer behavior.

External factors such as marketing campaigns, seasonality, competitor pricing, and customer demographics were not included.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that the business has strong overall profitability, but customer loyalty is relatively low and profit is highly concentrated among a small number of products and the Chocolate division.

The strongest growth opportunity is to increase customer purchase frequency, convert One-time customers into Repeat customers, develop high-value Repeat customers into Loyal customers, and protect the products and divisions that generate the majority of profit..
